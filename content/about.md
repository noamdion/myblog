Hi, I'm Ping. Welcome to my blog!

I live in Canada, and I am learning English as an adult. Learning English is not always easy, especially when it comes to listening and understanding native speakers. But I believe that if I keep learning and practicing, I will continue to improve.

I started this blog to share my thoughts about learning English, personal growth, books, and everyday life. I enjoy exploring new ideas and thinking about how we can live better lives.

Recently, I have been reading *Range* by David Epstein. It has made me think about learning, success, and the different paths people take in life. Sometimes, a book can teach us something important. Other times, it can make us question what we already believe.

I believe that simple ideas can have deep meaning. We do not always need complicated words to understand important things. Sometimes, the simplest explanation is the most powerful.

I am not an expert, and I do not have all the answers. I am just an ordinary person trying to learn, think, and grow.

Through this blog, I hope to share what I learn, express my honest thoughts, and connect with people who are also trying to understand the world around them.

My English may not always be perfect, but I will keep writing. After all, learning is a journey, not a competition.

Thanks for visiting my blog. I hope you find something here that makes you think, too.
