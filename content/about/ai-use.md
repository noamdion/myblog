I use AI tools in this blog, and I want to be clear about how.

## How I Use AI

- I use AI to help me check grammar and improve my English writing.
- I use AI to help me organize my ideas and structure my posts.
- I use AI to generate some of the cover images and illustrations on this site.
- I use AI to help me translate or explain difficult ideas.

## What AI Does Not Do

- AI does not write my posts for me. The ideas, experiences, and opinions are my own.
- AI does not decide what I write about.
- I review and edit everything before publishing.

## Why I Am Open About This

I am learning English as an adult. AI helps me express my thoughts more clearly and confidently. I believe it is important to be honest about how I use these tools.

Using AI does not replace my own thinking. It supports it.

If you have any questions about how I use AI, feel free to contact me.
