## The Pressure to Start Early

In China, many children are pushed too hard by their parents and schools. They often get good grades, but schools may not spend enough time teaching personal development, such as how to deal with other people, basic social etiquette, and problem-solving skills.

Because many students experience a lot of pressure for years, some of them may develop mental health problems. When they enter college or university, some are already tired of studying. They may not want to continue learning because they have spent so many years studying under pressure.

On the other hand, people who start later may have more opportunities to explore different things and learn through life experience. They may have tried different things and experienced failure before. Through these experiences, they may gradually understand what they really want. Their goals can become clearer because they have had more time to think about their lives.

## The Advantages and Disadvantages of Starting Later

The idea that we must figure out our entire lives by age twenty is mostly hype and exaggeration. We do not have to decide everything when we are young.

However, starting later also has disadvantages. Some people may feel lost when they see their peers becoming more successful than them. They may start asking themselves, "Am I doing something wrong? Am I not smart enough?"

Life is not about constantly comparing ourselves with other people. It is about comparing ourselves with who we were before and trying to become better. If we always compare ourselves with others, we can easily lose our own direction.

Because everything is changing so quickly nowadays, we need to keep learning. We should stay curious about the world, meet different people, make new friends, and try different hobbies. Life is not only about studying or being successful. We should also enjoy the process and remain open to new experiences.

Sometimes we feel tired, and that is okay. We can stop and take a break. Nobody should blame us for that. But we should not lose our direction. After we rest, we can get up and keep going.

## The Reality of Money and Time

But when we talk about starting early or starting later, we also need to consider reality.

For example, I am facing two choices in my own life. I can go back to my old job and start making money again, or I can continue my studies and try to find a new career after graduation.

If I go back to my old job, I can make money sooner. But if I continue my studies, I might spend another three years studying while earning less money. Even after I graduate, I still do not know whether I will be able to find a good job.

This is a real and practical problem. Exploration takes time and money. If you do not have enough money, you may not have the freedom to explore different things. You may have to focus on making a living first.

That is one reason why many Chinese people prefer to start early. Starting early allows them to earn money earlier and build a financial foundation. When unexpected problems happen in life, having that foundation may help them deal with those challenges.

Starting early can also be an advantage in some industries. For example, in the IT industry, some companies may prefer younger workers, especially for entry-level positions. People who start early may have more time to gain experience and build their careers.

For people who start later, building a professional foundation may be more difficult. They may need to spend more time developing their skills and becoming more competitive in the job market.

## Building a Foundation and Keeping Learning

In my opinion, starting early can be a good thing. We can build a foundation first, and then keep learning and exploring later.

However, starting early does not mean we should stop exploring. Some people start early but eventually become tired of learning. They may associate studying with stress and bad experiences, so they lose the motivation to continue.

On the other hand, people who start later may have more life experience and a clearer understanding of what they want. If they have a clear goal and genuinely want to learn, they may continue learning throughout their lives.

So, I think it is not only about when we start. It is also about whether we can keep learning over the long term.

Another book, *Atomic Habits*, offers a useful idea: we can build good habits and systems to help us achieve our goals. Exploration helps us discover what we really want, while good habits and systems help us keep working toward our goals.

I think we need both. We need opportunities to explore, but we also need a system that helps us make progress.

## What Does Success Really Mean?

After thinking about these ideas, I believe one of the important messages of *Range* is that we should not give up on learning and exploring.

For people who have already achieved success, continuing to learn, explore, and remain curious about the world is also important. Success is not only about what we have achieved. It is also about whether we can keep growing after achieving something.

We can never know for sure how our lives will turn out. We can make our best decisions, build good habits, and work toward our goals, but we cannot guarantee the results.

That is real life. Life is difficult, and there are no easy choices. Every choice has a cost.

So, whether we start early or start later, we need to be patient and believe in ourselves. If we try our best and still do not succeed, we do not need to blame ourselves. But if we never try, we may get stuck in a cycle of blaming ourselves and eventually lose our direction.

Once we know what we want to do and why we are doing it, we should take action instead of hesitating too much. We should keep learning, keep trying, and keep moving forward.

I hope everyone can find their own path in life, discover what they truly want, and live a life that is meaningful to them.
