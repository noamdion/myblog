I used to think that reading a book meant reading the words and understanding their meanings. However, I am beginning to realize that reading is also a skill that we need to develop.

## The Power of Annotation

Recently, I learned about annotation. When reading a book, we can underline important sentences, identify topic sentences, and mark cohesion devices. These methods help us understand how the author organizes ideas and connects sentences.

At first, this process can be slow and difficult. We cannot write down everything because there is not enough space on the page. We need to learn what is important and what is worth remembering.

## Making the Second Reading Easier

Although annotation takes extra time during the first reading, it can make the second reading much easier. Our notes help us remember important ideas and understand the text more deeply. Without them, we may forget what we have learned and struggle to understand the same passage again. 

## Reading is Thinking

I am also learning that reading is not just about learning English. It is about developing knowledge, understanding ideas, and learning to think more deeply. When we become better readers, we can learn more from books and connect new ideas with what we already know.

Reading well is not simply a matter of reading more books. It also means learning how to read effectively. The more we practise these skills, the more confident and independent readers we can become.
